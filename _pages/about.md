Hi, I'm Yifan! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Cornell University, advised by Dr. Vivek Srikrishnan in the [Srikrishnan Research Group](https://viveks.bee.cornell.edu/). My path here has been a winding one: I started out in biotechnology at Beijing Forestry University and forest sciences at the University of British Columbia, then found my way to water during my M.S. at the University of Michigan, modeling the Great Lakes and a groundwater contamination plume with Dr. Andrew Gronewold.

## Research

I study how climate, hydrology, institutions, and human decisions interact to shape water scarcity and adaptation. Two questions guide my work: How do environmental processes and societal choices shape vulnerability, resilience, and the distribution of risk? And how can that understanding inform policies and governance that support human well-being and environmental sustainability under uncertainty?

My doctoral research explores these questions in the western United States:

- **[Explainable AI for water shortage attribution](/project/proj5):** using deep learning emulators of a process-based hydrologic model and ensembles of explainable AI methods to identify what drives water shortages, and when those explanations can be trusted.
- **[Water markets and drought adaptation](/project/proj6):** testing whether water markets respond to scarcity as expected, and who gains or bears the costs when water is traded.
- **[Benchmarking explanation uncertainty](/project/proj7):** using the Fire Weather Index as a controlled benchmark to test whether explainable AI recovers known relationships.

I draw on environmental modeling, statistics and econometrics, machine learning, and decision science, paying close attention to how uncertainty, modeling assumptions, and societal values shape scientific conclusions and management choices.

**Research interests:** coupled human–Earth systems; climate impacts and adaptation; environmental institutions and governance; sustainable resource management; decision-making under uncertainty
